# Product Variants

Maps each OS-portfolio product to the case-study angle, hero metric, and sample pull-quote tone.

## Operational SaaS
**Products:** VehicleOS, FleetOS, FacilityOS, HomeOS, PetOS
**Hero angle:** Time and cost savings from eliminating manual tracking
**Primary metrics:**
- Hours saved per week/month
- Cost avoided (unplanned repairs, missed maintenance)
- Asset uptime %
- Records migrated from paper/spreadsheets
**Quote tone:** Practical, relief-oriented. "I stopped worrying about..."

## Compliance, Legal, Corporate Services
**Products:** SenangBoss, CorpOS, DebtOS
**Hero angle:** Professional-fee avoidance + audit-readiness
**Primary metrics:**
- Lawyer/CoSec hours replaced (× hourly rate = RM saved)
- Filing turnaround time (days → minutes)
- Audit finding reduction
- Compliance deadline hit rate
**Quote tone:** Confident, risk-mitigation framing. "We finally know we're compliant."

## Financial / Wealth
**Products:** EmasOS, WealthOS, RetireEmas/REMAS, InsurOS, Web3Insur
**Hero angle:** Access democratization + yield transparency
**Primary metrics:**
- AUM or holdings value
- Yield/return %
- Onboarding time (days → minutes)
- Customer acquisition cost reduction
**Quote tone:** Trust-oriented, conservative. Regulated-product language.

## Communication / Growth
**Products:** ChatOS, FunnelOS, MarketingOS, EventOS
**Hero angle:** Response velocity and conversion lift
**Primary metrics:**
- Response time (hours → minutes)
- Close rate improvement
- Lead volume handled per agent
- CAC reduction
**Quote tone:** Energetic, competitive. "We're winning deals we used to lose."

## Developer / Infrastructure
**Products:** McpOS, DB-OS, LogOS, SignalOS, AgentOS
**Hero angle:** Deploy velocity + reliability
**Primary metrics:**
- Deploy frequency
- Mean time to recovery
- Uptime %
- Engineering hours saved
**Quote tone:** Technical, specific. Developer-to-developer.

## Health / Longevity
**Products:** HealthOS, HealthCareOS, LongevityOS, GenSight
**Hero angle:** Outcome improvement + clinician time saved
**Primary metrics:**
- Patient outcome deltas
- Clinician minutes saved per case
- Adherence rate
**Quote tone:** Careful, outcome-focused. Avoid medical claims.
**Important:** Health case studies must use `[pending: clinical review]` for any outcome claim until verified.

## Learning / Training
**Products:** LearnOS, LearnChain (ReSkills)
**Hero angle:** Completion rate + credential portability
**Primary metrics:**
- Course completion %
- Time-to-certification
- Employer verification requests
**Quote tone:** Achievement-oriented from learner; ROI-oriented from employer.

## Web3 / Blockchain
**Products:** ChainOS, MYRX stablecoin, Web3Insur
**Hero angle:** Cost reduction vs traditional rails + transparency
**Primary metrics:**
- Settlement time
- Transaction cost vs fiat rails
- On-chain verifiability
**Quote tone:** Forward-looking but grounded. Avoid hype language.

## Default / Unmapped
If the product isn't listed, use the **Operational SaaS** template as the safe default, then adjust the metric section based on what the product actually does.
