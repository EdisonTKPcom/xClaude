# Customer Interview Script

A 15-minute call structure that extracts everything needed for a case study. Save output as `interview-{slug}.md` populated with customer answers.

## Pre-call prep (5 min)

- Pull the customer's usage data from D1/KV dashboard
- Note their plan, signup date, most-used features
- Come with 2-3 specific observations to anchor the conversation

## Call structure (15 min)

### 1. Context (2 min) — Who they are
- "Tell me briefly what [their company] does and your role there."
- "How big is the team using [product]?"

### 2. Before (3 min) — The pain
- "Before you started using [product], how were you handling [the problem]?"
- "What did that cost you — in time, money, or stress?"
- "Was there a specific moment that pushed you to look for a solution?"

### 3. Evaluation (1 min) — Why us
- "What made you pick [product] over alternatives?"

### 4. Implementation (2 min) — The rollout
- "Walk me through the first week. How long until you were actually using it?"
- "Did anything surprise you — good or bad — during setup?"

### 5. Now (4 min) — The payoff
- "What's different in your day-to-day now?"
- "Can you put a number on it? Hours saved, money saved, revenue gained?"
- "What's the single best thing [product] does for you?"

### 6. Quote (1 min) — The money line
- "If you were telling a peer in your industry about [product] in one sentence, what would you say?"
- *Note their exact words. Don't polish — that's the quote.*

### 7. Permission (1 min) — The ask
- "We'd love to write this up as a customer story. Which works for you: named with logo, anonymized by industry, or internal-only?"
- "Any metrics off-limits to share publicly?"

### 8. Close (1 min) — The favor
- "Would you be willing to [refer a peer / post a LinkedIn mention / join a 30-sec video testimonial]?"

## Post-call

Within 24 hours:
1. Write up answers into the interview template
2. Send draft case study to customer for approval (give them a 1-week deadline or it auto-approves with anonymization)
3. Flag any `[pending]` metrics you couldn't confirm verbally

## Red flags to watch for

- Customer hedges on every number → don't publish metrics, lead with qualitative quote instead
- Customer praises generic things ("great support!") but can't name specifics → interview isn't ready; schedule a follow-up
- Customer's pain-before is vague → push harder; a non-specific pain = non-specific case study
